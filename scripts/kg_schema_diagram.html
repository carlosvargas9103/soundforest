FOREST-KG ontology

One recording, many frames, two targets

The schema behind scripts/kg_ontology.ttl. Sound is one audio recording (18,567 total) and owns its (second, frequency-band) windows — Frame (626,130 total) — via hasFrame. The acoustic index values live on Frame; the link-prediction targets live on Sound, which is what gives a recording real structure to be predicted from — up to 4,490 Frame neighbors, not one.

Why not one Sound per frame? That was the first design, and it scored at chance level: predicting a content-based label from a single Sensor edge and a Band edge (identical across thousands of frames) carries almost no signal. Grouping frames under their recording gives each Sound a distinctive neighborhood shape to learn from instead.
