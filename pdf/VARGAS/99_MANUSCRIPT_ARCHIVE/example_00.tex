\begin{abstract}
% \todo{Enter your text here.}
The 2030 United Nations Agenda for Sustainable Development highlights the need to combat biodiversity loss and land degradation, which affect millions globally. This thesis aims to support the “Pagos por Servicios Ambientales” (PSA) environmental program as a case study. By leveraging 22,000 hours at over 100 sites in the Nicoya Peninsula of Costa Rica. This research applies deep learning techniques to classify bioacoustic signals of four different ecosystems under PSA. Using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs), the study aims to identify key sound features related to biodiversity and assess the efficacy of PSA in fostering ecological recovery. The resulting predictive model will contribute to improving biodiversity monitoring methods at large scales, offering insights into the role of sound-based analysis in environmental conservation.
\end{abstract}
